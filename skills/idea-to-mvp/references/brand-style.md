# Approved infographic brand

The approved infographic palette is bright warm yellow **`#FFE04A`**, warm cream **`#FAF8F2`**, and ink near-black **`#15130F`**. Apply it to English and Arabic infographics, including daily recaps and public skill explainers. This supersedes the earlier navy/purple/pastel direction.

- Use cream for the canvas, ink for titles/body text, and yellow for highlights, numbered steps, arrows, icons and key metrics. Supporting panels may use white, pale warm yellow or warm grayscale. Use ink text on yellow/cream; use yellow or cream text on an ink CTA.
- Carry the palette through illustration clothing, robots, document/graph accents and panel fills. Keep natural skin/hair. Preserve real supplied product screenshots faithfully rather than recoloring their interfaces.
- Use confident modern type, generous margins and clear editorial hierarchy. The reference typography direction is Schibsted Grotesk-style English and IBM Plex Sans Arabic-style Arabic. Keep Arabic shaped and RTL, and Western digits `0–9`, identifiers and URLs legible.
- A restrained sun-disc cue or yellow marker underline may be used when helpful. Preserve an already approved layout, content and illustration style when the request is a color correction. Do not add a portrait, brand name, cover headline, tagline, moon strip or unrelated reference text unless requested.
- Do not return to purple, lavender, indigo, navy, blue/cyan or mint/green as the default brand palette. A supplied real product screenshot or semantically necessary interface detail can retain its authentic colors.
- Use this as the default for these infographic templates. An explicit current request or another user's own brand overrides it; this does not impose the creator's branding on software products being built for someone else.

Only public visual specifications are recorded here. No source chat, device paths, account data, reference screenshots or private work are part of this brand note.
